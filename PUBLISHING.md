# Publishing policy

A public release must include:

- a clean source tree with no third-party GPL source copied into the core;
- pinned external revisions and license notes;
- versioned benchmark cases and schemas;
- machine-readable run manifests;
- exact commands for all primary figures and tables;
- failure cases and non-applicable regimes;
- a claim ledger linking prose claims to evidence.

The camera may control rendering detail, but it must not silently control the
physics partition. Screen-space refinement and physical refinement are separate
signals and must be logged separately.
