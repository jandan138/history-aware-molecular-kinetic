# Venue strategy — SIGGRAPH 2027 Conference first

## Target and shape

The active target is SIGGRAPH 2027 Technical Papers, provisionally the Conference
track if the target-year call retains the current dual-track structure. The exact
deadline, page limit, track names, and video rules must be checked when the 2027 CFP
is published.

Official program reference:

- https://s2026.siggraph.org/program/technical-papers/

The internal manuscript target is seven core pages plus up to two figure-only
pages. That is a production budget, not a claim about unpublished 2027 rules.

## Paper thesis

> **Molecular Time Machine: Authoring Physical Futures by Editing Collision
> History**

The paper's graphics contribution is a history-aware authoring representation and
workflow:

1. make binary collision events addressable and branchable;
2. recompute exact counterfactual futures after a past edit while reusing untouched
   history in the demonstrated branch;
3. navigate backward from a selected future feature to a useful past cause;
4. preserve one declared visible present while editing hidden velocity ownership to
   choose a future;
5. expose the workflow through a polished 3D cinematic and interactive companion.

## Active evidence gate

The SIG route is supported by the frozen E1–E5 chain:

- E1 `go`: a frame is not a future at the declared resolved state;
- E2 `stop_e2`: a collision-dose slider is not special causal wiring;
- E3 `go`: one addressable collision creates an exact alternate branch;
- E4 `go`: a desired consequence can locate and steer a past cause;
- E5 `go`: four particles preserve the declared present and selectively change the
  future glyph.

E2's negative mechanism result is the plot turn, not a missing acceptance gate.
The paper does not require E2 wiring to pass, two edit families, broad scaling,
generic locality, a user study, or a new 3D physics solver.

## Production gate

Before E7 manuscript freeze:

- all primary figures and video shots must consume frozen artifacts;
- the E5 signature Hero and the real-time companion must consume the same shot
  bundle;
- branch comparisons must use locked camera, time, materials, and post-processing;
- all visible metrics must be read from evidence artifacts;
- the wording must remain `same declared 4×2 resolved present`, not same exact
  microstate;
- every active claim must map to a run, metric artifact, camera hash, and renderer
  manifest.

## IEEE VIS

VIS remains a future re-framing option, not a parallel E6 deliverable. A VIS paper
would require a different research question, domain-task analysis, linked-view
design rationale, and a user or expert study. None of those are added to protect
the current SIG submission.
