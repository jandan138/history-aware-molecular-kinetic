# Benchmark freezing

## Candidate

May change freely but cannot support a final claim.

## Review checklist

- units and model assumptions;
- initial ensemble;
- geometry and boundaries;
- resolutions and seed family;
- oracle/reference;
- metrics and thresholds;
- applicability and failure labels;
- licenses and redistribution.

## Frozen

Content hash and case ID are immutable. Parameter sweeps reference the frozen
base and explicit overrides.

## Retired

A retired case remains discoverable with its flaw, affected evidence, and
replacement. Deleting failed benchmark history is prohibited.
