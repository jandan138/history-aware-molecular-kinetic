# Canonical cases

## C0 — Two-body collision

Analytic EDMD correctness.

## C1 — Periodic equilibrium

Dilute and moderate packing fractions. Measures invariants, pressure, collision
rate, and \(g(r)\).

## C2 — Anisotropic relaxation

Homogeneous non-Maxwellian velocity distribution. Separates kinetic relaxation
from geometry.

## C3 — Two-color mixing chamber

Species transport with a removable divider. Also serves as a zoomable demo.

## C4 — Narrow cavity

Reflective confinement and repeated wall returns.

## C5 — Correlation labyrinth

Parallel paths, recirculating pockets, and matched local-state sampling regions.
Designed to test history beyond state.

## C6 — Moving-wall shear

Anisotropic stress, heat generation where applicable, and collision statistics.

## C7 — Expansion into vacuum

Time-dependent transition from collisional chamber to ballistic plume.

## C8 — Static exact/kinetic interface

Conversion and flux without dynamic policy.

## C9 — Moving refinement region

A simple translating plate/cylinder moves a candidate exact region.

## Scene-design rule

Every geometry element must exist to expose a declared model or algorithmic
question. Decorative complexity is deferred to B5 and cannot alter physics.
