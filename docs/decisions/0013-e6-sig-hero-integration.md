# ADR 0013 — E6 is the SIG Hero integration, not another experiment suite

**Status:** accepted.

## Context

E1–E5 already close the active scientific and creator-facing argument:

```text
E1  a frame is not a future
E2  a slider is not a history
E3  one past collision is editable
E4  a desired future can locate its cause
E5  the same resolved visible present can be authored into a chosen future
```

The former E6 outline mixed new baselines, seed and scale sweeps, additional edit
families, failure reels, performance engineering, 3D physics, and final production.
That scope would dilute the paper after its central authoring result is already
known.

## Decision

E6 is one integrated graphics deliverable:

> **Turn the frozen E1–E5 evidence into a SIG-quality Molecular Time Machine whose
> signature moment is Same Present, Chosen Future.**

The primary Hero keeps the E5 `N=256`, seed-4 recipe and its registered `t=0.80`
pivot. It presents the 30 legal previews, the two velocity-ownership swaps
`(48,82)` and `(174,211)`, and the matched E/C-like futures as a continuous authoring
session. The renderer reads frozen trajectories and metrics; it never changes the
simulation.

All primary footage uses a three-dimensional presentation of the existing planar
hard-disk data. Particle centres remain on the physical plane. Three-dimensional
materials, cameras, lighting, trails, and compositing are presentation variables,
not a new 3D hard-sphere claim.

E6 uses a renderer-neutral shot bundle with two consumers:

- Blender Cycles GPU for the cinematic master and paper stills; E6-v0 uses
  explicit CUDA in WSL previews, while the 4K final batch runs in native Windows
  Blender because the WSL OptiX/DXG bridge is not production-stable;
- a self-contained Three.js companion for interactive browsing of cached exact
  trajectories.

Genesis remains a neutral local validator. Its renderer is not allowed to become a
dependency of the physics package or the final-quality bottleneck.

## Consequences

- E2 appears once as the narrative turn from dose sliders to causal history. Its
  closed mechanism claim is not reopened.
- No seed grid, density sweep, new baseline implementation, random-surgery control,
  second edit family, geometry edit, user study, failure reel, or 3D solver belongs
  to E6.
- Deterministic regeneration of frozen trajectories and renderer-integrity checks
  are allowed; they do not create new scientific claims.
- The final paper title is provisionally **Molecular Time Machine: Authoring
  Physical Futures by Editing Collision History**.
- E6 closes the visual-evidence and demonstrated-workflow gates. E7 is the separate
  manuscript and submission-package stage.
