# ADR 0012 — E5 is Same Present, Chosen Future

**Status:** accepted.

## Context

E1 showed that a declared resolved present omits collision correlation that can
change its future. E3 made one past collision editable, and E4 let a creator begin
with a desired terminal feature and locate a useful past cause. The remaining paper
gap is to turn E1's information boundary itself into a creator operation.

The previous E5 outline mixed generic block chaotization, moment-matching
refinement, partial history controls, random baselines, and multiple scenes. That
would mostly audit whether a surgery is harmless. It would not give the paper one
clear authoring climax.

## Decision

E5 is one registered Hero:

> **Keep one visible present fixed, exchange hidden velocity ownership, and choose
> whether its future resolves into E or C.**

The creator first selects the horizontal extension of the future Molecular E middle
stroke. The system returns to the `N=256`, seed-4 reverse branch at `t=0.80`. It
enumerates only one or two disjoint velocity transpositions between selected target
particles occupying the same declared `4×2` spatial cell. Every candidate receives
a complete EDMD preview from that common pivot.

Candidates must retain at least 90% of the non-target glyph. Among them, selection
maximizes target-particle ejection, then collateral retention, then edit sparsity.
The frozen palette contains 30 candidates. Calibration selects swaps `(48,82)` and
`(174,211)`; the clean recipe run remains the formal evidence.

## Why this operator preserves the declared present

The surgery changes no position, passive color, radius, mass, particle ID, or
weight. Exchanging two velocity values within one declared cell leaves that cell's
velocity multiset unchanged. Restricting both particles to the creator-selected
target also preserves the target-conditioned cellwise velocity multiset. Global
mass, momentum, and energy are therefore preserved up to floating-point summation.

This supports **same declared `4×2` resolved present**. It does not support same
exact microstate or equality at arbitrary resolution.

## Consequences

- E5 is an outcome-conditioned correlation surgery, not a history-retention slider.
- The registered target is chosen before the candidate surgery is selected.
- Candidate outcome evaluation is allowed: E5 is explicitly an authoring search
  over a compact physical preview palette, unlike E4's baseline-only causal ranking.
- All previews use complete EDMD after the pivot. E5 makes no local-speed claim.
- The renderer never removes middle-stroke particles or corrects their positions.
- No seed grid, particle-count sweep, random-surgery baseline, DSMC comparison,
  geometry edit, user study, 3D scene, or native rewrite belongs to the E5 gate.

## Story consequence

The active paper spine becomes:

```text
E1: a frame is not a future
E2: a slider is not a history
E3: one past collision is editable
E4: a desired future can locate its cause
E5: the same visible present can be authored into a chosen future
```
