# Visual Acceptance Criteria — Molecular Echoes

These criteria turn “fancy demo” into auditable E6 evidence. Thresholds may be tuned
while a case is candidate, but may not be changed per method after freezing.

## 1. Evidence integrity

Every primary frame/clip must identify:

- case ID;
- parent and child branch IDs;
- fork time and edit ID;
- checkpoint ID;
- simulation commit and configuration hash;
- input/output artifact hashes;
- frame time and camera hash;
- renderer/comparison-lock hash;
- linked quantitative metric.

A branch after its fork must be generated by forward simulation. A reversed frame
sequence may appear only as a labeled explanatory baseline.

## 2. Shared-renderer fairness

Primary comparisons freeze:

- camera and projection;
- frame times and temporal alignment;
- particle display radius/density;
- passive-color mapping;
- trail length and motion blur;
- lighting and tone mapping;
- geometry opacity;
- graph/causal-cone overlay policy;
- output resolution and crop.

No branch may receive method-specific denoising, particle deletion, retiming, or
manual trajectory cleanup.

## 3. Exact reversal and replay visibility

### Required

- exact reverse and deterministic replay are identified separately;
- physical reverse error is linked to the displayed run;
- replay divergence, if any, is shown or causes the shot to fail;
- the video never implies that history storage is what makes Newtonian dynamics
  reversible.

### Fail

- footage looks correct only because frames were played backward;
- the solver snaps particles to recorded states without disclosure;
- exact and extended dynamics are visually conflated.

## 4. Same-resolved-present acceptance

At the pivot:

- exact-reverse and chaotized branches use the same camera/time;
- the registered `f1_h`/moment audit passes;
- at least one finer spatial and velocity audit is reported;
- visible current-frame difference is subordinate to the later branch difference;
- wording says “same resolved present,” not “same exact state.”

A shot fails if the future can be predicted simply from an obvious pivot mismatch.

## 5. History-mechanism acceptance

A history-budget shot must include or link:

- full EDMD;
- structured `(Lambda,Gamma)` budget;
- count/time-matched random suppression;
- topology-shuffled or ghost control;
- collision-count statistics;
- the declared future observable.

A pretty slider is insufficient if structured and random controls produce the same
response.

## 6. Counterfactual branch correctness

For every primary edit shot:

- local branch and full-resimulation reference share the same fork/edit manifest;
- terminal state/event error is below the declared tolerance;
- affected-particle/event fraction is synchronized with the animation;
- fallback to full replay is visibly and numerically recorded;
- parent branch remains immutable.

Approximate bounded-cone methods require a separate label and error curve.

## 7. Temporal quality

Measure frame-to-frame change in a neutral render:

\[
D_t = \|I_{t+\Delta t}-I_t\|.
\]

Fork, checkpoint restore, and overlay transitions must not create discontinuities
that dominate natural particle motion unless the physical edit itself is
instantaneous and declared.

Required checks:

- persistent particle identity in exact branches;
- no display-particle boiling used to dramatize branch difference;
- graph layout is stable enough to follow ancestry;
- passive-color mass does not pop;
- branch time is aligned.

## 8. Hero-scene gates

### Molecular Logo Echo

Pass when:

- multi-resolution pivot audit passes;
- exact/chaotized future difference is obvious under shared rendering;
- exact branch is forward-simulated after velocity reversal;
- history-budget result survives null controls;
- the pattern is not manually reconstructed.

### One Collision, Two Worlds

Pass when:

- one registered edit produces a real fork;
- local branch matches full resimulation;
- causal influence begins locally and is understandable;
- affected fraction and runtime/storage are linked;
- graph overlay explains the branch but is not the only result.

### Edit the Past

Pass when:

- geometry revision and checkpoint are explicit;
- unaffected history is genuinely reused;
- local/full edited futures agree;
- at least one output such as exit flux, residence, or color transport changes;
- a global-cone failure case is included.

## 9. Graphics-value gate

At least two of the following must be immediately legible without debug overlays:

- past pattern recovery versus continued mixing;
- two futures diverging from one selected collision;
- geometry edit changing a later transport outcome;
- local branch matching the global edited reference;
- same current resolved view leading to intentionally different futures.

If only the graph, mask, or numeric inset differs, the SIG graphics claim fails.

## 10. Claim-to-pixel integrity

Every shot must answer one question and map to the active claim ledger. Hero styling
may improve readability, but neutral and hero renders must reach the same scientific
conclusion.

## 11. Camera and interaction firewall

Camera navigation, graph selection, and display density may not alter:

- physical branch state;
- fork/edit semantics;
- collision history;
- causal-cone membership;
- surgery result;
- history budget.

Interaction changes physics only through a recorded edit manifest.

## 12. Failure reel

The supplementary video must include:

- numerical reversal failure/limit;
- coarse `f1_h` audit that becomes distinguishable at finer resolution;
- count-matched control explaining a candidate budget result;
- causal cone becoming global;
- local branch fallback;
- surgery causing current-frame mismatch;
- dense or ambiguous collision ordering case.
